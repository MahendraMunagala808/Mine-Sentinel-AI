About MineSentinel AI
Intelligent IoT & Predictive Mine Safety Platform
Edge IoT + Machine Learning Safety Intelligence

MineSentinel AI is an automated industrial safety platform engineered for hazardous underground coal mines, mineral extraction sites, and industrial tunnels. It continuously monitors hazardous atmospheric telemetry and executes early emergency alerts before dangerous conditions escalate.

Edge Sensor Array

ESP32 Dev Module paired with 16-bit ADS1115 ADC ingesting high-precision analog feeds from MQ-2 (Combustible Gas), MQ-7 (Carbon Monoxide), DHT11 (Temp/Humidity), and Optical Flame sensors.

Predictive AI Classifier

Multi-class Random Forest model evaluating sensor telemetry in real time to classify environmental risks as Safe, Warning, or Critical, triggering automated fan relays and sirens.

Core Objectives & System Capabilities
  • Continuous real-time sampling and edge-to-cloud MQTT streaming every 2.5 seconds.
  • Instant multi-tier visual and audible dispatch triggers for toxic gas accumulation or fire outbreaks.
  • Permanent SQLite historical audit logging with one-click full CSV dataset exports.
  • Local network fault-tolerance with automated offline edge recovery.

Edge-First Data Architecture

All environmental telemetry, gas concentration streams, and risk classifications are processed and stored locally on your network edge. No telemetry is shared with external commercial advertising or analytics brokers.

1. Sensor Telemetry & Storage

Live readings from MQ-2 (Combustible Gas), MQ-7 (Carbon Monoxide), DHT11 (Temperature & Humidity), and Optical Flame sensors are logged directly into the local SQLite database for historical safety analytics.

2. Operator Session Information

Operator authentication data is restricted solely to session authorization, shift auditing, and emergency dispatch accountability.

3. Data Ownership & Export

Your organization retains 100% ownership of all captured mining safety logs. Complete datasets can be exported in structured CSV format at any time directly from the operations dashboard.

Operational Terms & Safety Protocols
Guidelines for Underground Telemetry & Emergency Escalation
Primary Safety Directive

MineSentinel AI is an automated early-warning intelligence aid. In any Critical hazard event (Toxic Gas Leak, Fire, Severe Thermal Breach), physical evacuation and emergency siren protocols must be executed immediately without delay.

1. Sensor Calibration & Health

Microcontroller edge nodes (ESP32 + ADS1115 ADC) must undergo scheduled diagnostic testing and baseline recalibration in accordance with mine safety standards.

2. Predictive AI Risk Scoring

The integrated Machine Learning risk model evaluates compound sensor matrices in real time (Safe, Warning, Critical) to assist mine supervisors in identifying micro-shifts before catastrophic threshold breaches occur.

3. Alert Dispatch Responsibilities

Operators on active watch must acknowledge warning states, monitor ventilation fan triggers, and log resolution notes within the event management console.
