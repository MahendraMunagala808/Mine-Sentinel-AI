Overview

Comprehensive Underground Intelligence

MineSentinel AI bridges the gap between dangerous physical mining conditions and proactive industrial intelligence. By uniting hardware IoT telemetry with cloud deep learning, the platform detects micro-shifts in environmental toxicity, heat, and flame long before catastrophic failure occurs.

Edge Telemetry Ingestion

Continuous sampling of toxic gas concentration (MQ-2, MQ-7), ambient temperature, humidity (DHT11), and optical flame presence via ultra-low latency NodeMCU microcontrollers paired with 16-bit high-precision ADS1115 ADCs.

AI Predictive Risk Engine

Machine learning models continuously evaluate multi-parameter sensor arrays to calculate dynamic risk scores, identifying compound hazards like explosive gas accumulation and sudden carbon monoxide spikes.

Autonomous Alarm Dispatch

Instantaneous multi-channel siren triggers and visual notifications when environmental safety thresholds are breached, ensuring mine personnel receive immediate evacuation alerts.

Live Telemetry Analytics

Interactive real-time visualization dashboards featuring dynamic metric charts, active alert centers, system state monitoring, and automated safety audit logging.
